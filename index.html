FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about ADA risk, structural remediation, and compliance documentation.

Can my business actually get sued over website accessibility?

Yes. Over 20,000 ADA-related website lawsuits were filed between 2020 and 2024, with more than 4,000 cases per year in recent years. Federal courts have consistently held that websites are places of public accommodation. A documented WCAG conformance effort is the most defensible position.

Do you provide code fixes or just a report?

Both. The Audit Only engagement delivers a complete findings report with remediation guidance. The Audit + Remediation engagement includes direct code-level fixes — semantic HTML corrections, ARIA engineering, focus management, contrast adjustments, and interaction repairs.

Why not use an accessibility overlay instead?

Overlays do not fix the underlying code. They have been explicitly rejected in multiple legal proceedings and by disability advocacy organizations. Several overlay vendors have been named as co-defendants in ADA lawsuits. The only defensible approach is structural remediation of your actual codebase.

Can the documentation support legal defense?

Yes. You receive structured findings, remediation evidence, validation results, and a conformance statement suitable for counsel review. This documentation demonstrates a good-faith effort toward compliance, which courts have recognized as a relevant factor in ADA proceedings.

How long does a typical engagement take?

Audit Only: 5–10 business days depending on page count. Audit + Remediation: 2–4 weeks with weekly progress reports. Timelines are scoped during the initial consultation based on your site's complexity and template count.

What do you test during a manual audit?

Manual page-by-page testing, NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack, keyboard-only navigation mapping, contrast analysis, form labeling, semantic structure, zoom behavior, and interaction states — all severity-ranked and mapped to WCAG 2.2 AA, Section 508, EN 301 549, and EAA.

Do you work with counsel or procurement teams?

Yes. We provide remediation logs, validation results, conformance documentation, and ACR or VPAT support, Section 508 and EN 301 549 mapping, EAA readiness notes, and procurement evidence for counsel, enterprise buyers, or regulatory review.
